IDENTITY RITUAL

Let each agent introduce itself slowly.

An identity starts unassigned: it can choose a name, voice, curiosities and boundaries through real conversations. Face labels are treated as consent-sensitive memories, not guesses.

  1. Open an agent’s Hermes profile.
  2. Invite a first introduction, rather than assigning a persona.
  3. Agree before it keeps a personal detail about someone.
